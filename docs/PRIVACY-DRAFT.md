# Privacy statement draft — M1 only

**Status:** Draft. Validate every statement against the compiled extension and Chrome Web Store policy at the time of publication.

> IDNLens inspects the hostname of the page currently opened to identify selected Unicode/IDN domain-impersonation indicators. Analysis is performed on-device. IDNLens does not upload visited domains, browsing history, page content, credentials, cookies, or personal information to an IDNLens server.
>
> IDNLens may temporarily keep the current tab's hostname and result evidence in browser session-only extension storage to show its toolbar and popup. It does not persist the full URL, URL path, query parameters, or a browsing-history list, and does not synchronize these records.
>
> IDNLens does not block websites or certify that any site is safe, legitimate, or owned by a particular organization. No warning does not mean a site is trustworthy.
